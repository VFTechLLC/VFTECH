How build pricing works

Four rules that hold on every build, whatever the tier.

Payment schedule

Payment in thirds

Fixed fee
  1. One third at signing
  2. One third at design approvalScope freezes
  3. One third at launch

Scope freezes at design approval. Any change after that is quoted to you in writing first.

Fixed fee, never hourly

Your proposal gives a fixed fee, a fixed scope and a date, with the risks named up front.

Discounts are shown, never buried

If your rate is below standard, your quote line-items the standard price and shows the credit.

Monthly plans

Augmented support and maintenance: ongoing care where AI does the first pass and a human owns the outcome.

One fee covers hosting, security, backups and availability. No separate hosting bills, and no second vendor for you to manage.

You get faster turnaround than a traditional agency at a comparable price. Standard and Priority add the AI update assistant, so the changes you shouldn’t need to call about become self-service, with a human approving every change before it goes live.
