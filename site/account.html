How it works

A code instead of a password.

Email delivery and the backend must be configured before live account creation is enabled. Once enabled, enter your email and complete CAPTCHA verification. A six-digit code expires after ten minutes and can be used once. Verifying it creates your customer account or signs you into an existing one. The portal shows only requests submitted with your verified email address.

Account sessions use a private browser cookie. Signing out ends your session. The public demos use sample identities and do not create real accounts, activate paid plans or process payments.
