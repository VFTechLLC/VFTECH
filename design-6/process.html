Seven steps, start to finish

From a free conversation to a handover you could leave at, then a support plan only if you want one.

  1. Conversation

    30 minutes, free. What’s broken, what it’s costing you, and whether this is the right firm for it.

  2. Assessment

    A written audit of your current state: what exists, what’s wrong, what it would take. Credited against the build if you proceed.

  3. Proposal

    Fixed fee, fixed scope, a date, and the risks named rather than discovered later.

  4. Build

    A working preview from the first week. Weekly written updates. One consolidated round of feedback per milestone. Why both matter.

  5. Cutover

    Tested with live data before the switch, monitored after, with a documented rollback.

  6. Handover

    Code, accounts, credentials and documentation transferred to you. You could leave at this point and be fine. The exit terms.

  7. Ongoing

    A support plan, or nothing at all. Both are acceptable outcomes. See the monthly plans.

The exit terms, in full

Every engagement, at every price, includes all of it. None of it is an upsell.

Code
The complete source code, in a repository you own.
Accounts
Every account — hosting, database, domain, email — in your own name from day one.
Data
All content and data in standard, portable formats.
Documentation
Written documentation of how the system is built, deployed and maintained.
Transition
Thirty days of transition support at no charge if you ever leave, including working directly with whoever takes over.
  • No exit fee.
  • No data-release charge.
  • No notice period beyond the 30 days on a support plan.
