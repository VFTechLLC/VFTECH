    Questions

    What people ask before the first call.

    What does the free conversation cover?

    Thirty minutes on what’s broken, what it’s costing you, and whether this is the right firm for it. If it isn’t, you’ll hear that too.

    Will I really own everything?

    Yes, on every engagement at every price: the complete source code in a repository you own, every account (hosting, database, domain, email) in your name from day one, all content and data in standard formats, and written documentation of how it’s built, deployed and maintained.

    What happens if I want to leave?

    No exit fee and no data-release charge. You get thirty days of transition support at no charge, including working directly with whoever takes over. The test is simple: if we disappeared tomorrow, someone else could keep your systems running.

    How is a rebuild priced?

    A fixed fee, never hourly, from $3,500 for a Launch site. The audit comes first, then your fixed quote. You pay in thirds: at signing, at design approval and at launch. Scope freezes at design approval, and any change after that is quoted to you in writing first.

    Does AI change my site on its own?

    No. AI does the first pass; a human approves every change before it goes live.

    Do I have to take a monthly plan?

    No. After handover it’s a support plan or nothing at all, and both are acceptable outcomes. Plans run from $350 a month and include hosting, security, backups and availability.

    Who does the work?

    Kevin Van Flandern: 25 years building and delivering software, co-founder and CTO of Roundit, three tours at Microsoft and four U.S. patents.
