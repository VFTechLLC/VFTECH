Customer account

Can’t sign in?

Two ways back into your VFTech account. Neither needs a password.

Get a sign-in code

Enter the email on your account. If there’s an account for it, we email you a six-digit code.

Signed up with Google? Use Continue with Google on the sign-in page. It opens the same account as your email.

Account help is not switched on yet.

The code expires after ten minutes and works once.

Lost your two-factor phone?

Sign in as usual, then choose Use a recovery code instead and enter one of the ten codes you saved when you turned two-factor on. Each code works once. Once you’re in, turn two-factor off and on again from your account page to move it to your new phone.

No recovery codes left? Use the form below and say you’ve lost your two-factor device. We check who you are the same way before switching two-factor off, end every sign-in, and email you when it’s done.

Lost access to your email?

Tell us a new email you can use and something we can check. We verify it against our records before changing anything, then reply to the new address.
