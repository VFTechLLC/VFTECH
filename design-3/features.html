Your guide, right on the page.

The VFTech guide answers locally from text bundled with this site. It quotes published page content and links to its source. It does not send questions to an AI service, search the internet or read linked websites. It declines questions it cannot match to the site. Chat remains in this tab. After the guide files load, answers work without an internet connection. It is a local text guide, not a model trained on private customer data.

With “Follow this page” enabled, clicking a demo or section updates the guide’s About button. It reads control and section names, never the contents of your contact or account form.

A request that reaches the right person.

The contact form prepares a request you can copy. Email delivery remains disabled until the business email and backend are configured. When enabled, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile verification, validates your details and sends the request to the configured business inbox. A submission is marked sent only after the email provider accepts it. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive documents.
