Terms for monthly plans

Placeholder terms, pending legal review. They describe how billing works today; the reviewed version will replace them.

Monthly plans

  • Plans (Essentials, Standard, Priority) are billed monthly in advance, in US dollars, at the price published on this site when you subscribe.
  • Sales tax is added where it applies and shown on each invoice.
  • Website builds and other projects are quoted separately and are not covered by these terms.

One-time purchases

  • Fixed-price builds bought online (for example Launch) are charged once at checkout, plus tax where it applies. The receipt appears in your account.
  • Quoted work is invoiced separately once the price is agreed.

Cancelling a plan

  • You can cancel any time from your account (Manage billing). Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, then stops.
  • Payments are not refunded, in full or in part, including for the rest of a month after you cancel.

Payments

  • Payments are processed by Stripe. VFTech never sees or stores your full card number.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe retries it and emails you; the plan may pause if it can't be collected.

Your account data

  • We keep your name, email address, the requests you send us, and your plan and invoice status, to provide the service.
  • We don't sell your data. Stripe keeps your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  • To ask about or remove your data, contact us.
